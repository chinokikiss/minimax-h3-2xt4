## E2E completion-first error policy — 2026-11-04

**Prioritize completing the full E2E run and retaining its outputs over fail-fast diagnostic checks.** For all new or modified E2E benchmarks, notebooks, and supervisors, non-critical errors, warnings, observer/telemetry failures, and post-run validation/assertion mismatches must be recorded with the stage, exception/traceback, and relevant context, **without forcibly exiting or aborting remaining feasible stages**. In particular, a successful decoder/export must not be declared a hard failure solely because a diagnostic strategy-name allowlist or non-essential assertion is outdated. Report such runs as completed with recorded errors/warnings rather than silently calling every check PASS.

Only terminate early for **genuinely fatal** conditions that make meaningful execution impossible or unsafe (e.g., unrecoverable CUDA OOM/device failure, unavailable required model/input, irrecoverable invalid tensor/state, or a data-integrity/security risk). Do not suppress these failures, fabricate outputs, or continue with corrupted data. Preserve partial artifacts and full diagnostics in either case. Apply this policy prospectively; do not rewrite historical failure receipts or change existing Kaggle execution/submission permissions.
