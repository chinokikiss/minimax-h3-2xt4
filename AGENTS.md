### Entry, configuration, model residency, and sampling

1. **Real ComfyUI entry:** `custom_nodes/minimax_h3_tp2/__init__.py::MiniMaxH3TP2Loader` creates `RuntimeConfig` with `H3OperatorPolicy` and `TensorFlowPolicy`, then `PhysicalTPService` from `patches/comfy_tp_runtime.py`. This is the public user-facing route. The loader also exposes the VAE loader and opt-in PTD nodes; these are separate execution concerns.
2. **Runtime / workers:** `patches/comfy_tp_runtime.py` owns the persistent two-rank worker, NFE-specific attention selection, weight admission, lookahead, sampler and decode requests. `patches/comfy_sampler.py` integrates the service with the pinned legacy ComfyUI hooks; `patches/attention_schedule.py` parses the optional 1-based Kitchen/Sol schedule.
3. **Block construction (critical):** `patches/windowed_tp.py::prepare_tp_block` loads shards with `patches/rank_local_int8.py` (or the qualified offline rank-block loader), attaches online LoRA if requested, calls `patches/h3_operator_policy.py::configure_block`, then `patches/h3_tensor_flow.py::prepare_tp`. `patches/weight_residency.py` transfers/copies these configured modules into the GPU window. Do not patch only an obsolete loader or a benchmark-created block.
4. **Actual blocks:** `patches/tp_qwen3vl.py` for TP2 text encoder (TE), `patches/tp_dit.py::TPDiTBlockINT8` for denoising DiT; the latter calls `ColumnParallelLinearINT8` for QKV/FC1 and `RowParallelLinearINT8` for out_proj/FC2. `patches/optimized_tp.py::enable` installs the guarded TP2 row/collective implementations, but does **not** replace Kitchen's `int8_linear` registry implementation.
5. **Default numerical/storage policy:** Loader `tensor_flow='fp16'` and `residual_exponent=9` mean FP16 TE/DiT residual stream scaled by **1/512**; FP32 islands are retained for sensitive arithmetic. `RuntimeConfig` also defaults to FP16 flow. `TensorFlowPolicy()` alone defaults to `legacy` for direct component callers; do not mistake that class constructor for the ComfyUI production default. Vision and Video VAE residual exponents independently default to 0; Audio VAE is separate native FP32.
